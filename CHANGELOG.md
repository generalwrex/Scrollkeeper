# Scrollkeeper

## 1.0.0 – first real version

Everything for the mage's Comprehension skill in one place.

**The panel**
- **One click for everything:** Decipher, Study, Open bundle, and Familiar and
  Imbue buttons that use your best scroll (a tick shows when it's already up).
- **Three views:** Advanced (network, community odds and your stats built in),
  Simple, and Title only (just the title and your skill bar). The map-style
  arrow button switches between them.
- **Your Scrolls in categories:** Imbues, Familiars, Combat, Utility and Upgrades,
  each with its skill-up color. The best imbue for the weapon you're holding is
  outlined in gold. Scrolls you can't use yet are dimmed, and clicking one tells
  you why (level too low, wrong weapon) without using it.
- **Imbue help:** if a weapon in your bags fits one of your imbues, the button
  says "swap weapon" and the tooltip names the weapon to equip.
- **Combat:** the panel hides when a fight starts and comes back after (you can
  turn that off), and the X always closes it, even in combat.

**Deciphering and Study**
- Skill-up colors on every scroll; the Decipher button reads skill-up scrolls first.
- Study timer for each of your mages, and the Study button wakes up when it's ready.
- Libraries are detected when you stand near the librarian, and both known ones
  (Undercity and Stormwind) are pinned on the map.

**Sharing with other players**
- Scrollkeeper users in your guild and group share what each scroll tier turns
  into (Community Odds), who needs which scrolls, libraries, and where scrolls
  and librarian books drop.
- **Community data ships with the addon,** so you see odds and drop spots even
  with nobody else online. Share yours with `/sk export`.
- World map pins for libraries, scroll drops and book finds; click a library
  pin for a waypoint (TomTom if you have it).

**Books for the librarians**
- Learned when you hand one in, shared with other users, and where they're
  found goes on the map.

**Alts**
- Every character knows your mages. Scroll tooltips on any character say which
  of your mages can read it, and non-mages get a small window with each mage's
  skill and Study timer.

**Everywhere else**
- Scroll names are item links in chat, tooltips and the panel: hover for the
  tooltip, Shift-click to link it.
- Reminders: no weapon imbue, no familiar out, a Scroll of Comprehension that
  would unlock more, weapon upgrades that are ready.
- Titan Panel / LibDataBroker entry, addon menu entry, Auctionator prices for
  non-mages.
- **About page** in Options > AddOns > Scrollkeeper, and `/sk about`.
- `/sk bug` copies a bug report with a ready-made title for GitHub.

Earlier test versions (0.1 to 0.5.3) built up to this release.
