I got tired of digging through my bags every time I wanted to decipher a scroll, so I made this. Scrollkeeper puts your whole Comprehension routine for WoW Forever in one window, and it tells you which scrolls will actually raise your skill before you waste them.

The part I'm proudest of though is that it connects mages. Your alts, guildies, friends and everyone else running it help each other out. Most of that stuff no other scroll addon does.

## Your Scroll Bag

Deciphered scrolls pile up fast. `/sk bag` (or the bag button on the panel) shows every one you own, counted across your bags, bank and mail.

*   Each scroll is **Keep**, **Extra** (more than you want) or **Set aside** (an imbue for a weapon you don't have). Change how many to keep with - and +, or right-click a scroll for keep all, protect, pin and more
*   **Presets** for how many to keep (lean, raid-ready, or keep everything), and save your own with **New preset**
*   **Stack**, **Sell extras** at a vendor, **Mail extras** to your bank alt, and **Deposit set aside** at the bank. Protected scrolls are never sold, mailed or deleted
*   Right-click the panel's Familiar or Imbue button for all your scrolls of that kind, and star one to **pin** it. Imbues get a pin per weapon, so swapping to a dagger picks your dagger imbue
*   Colored marks on scrolls in your bags, and Keep / Extra / Set aside sections in your bag addon
*   Got a new weapon? It tells you which imbues for it are waiting in your bank or mail

## Getting scrolls to the right person

*   **Fellow Mages** (`/sk net`, or right on the panel in Advanced view). Every mage running Scrollkeeper on your ruleset (it says which: "Fellow PvE Mages"), with their skill and which tiers they still need. Mages you've seen this week stay listed (grayed) after they log off, so you can still mail them, and players on another class show their mage. Hover a name to see everything, including how many of the scrolls in your bags they could use. Click to whisper them, Shift-click to drop their name in chat or the mail. Sort by skill, name, or who could use the most of your scrolls (right-click that one to only show those mages).
*   **At the mailbox** there's a list of who could use the scrolls you're carrying. Your own mages first, then guildies, friends and group members running Scrollkeeper. Click a name and it fills in the mail, attaches their scrolls and writes a short note. It never sends, you still click Send. There's a setting to also offer scrolls you'd still use yourself.
*   **Scroll swap board** (`/sk swap`). See who has spare scrolls you'd level from, and which mages would level from yours. Click a name to whisper them.
*   **In the guild bank** scrolls get a little colored dot if you, one of your mages, or a guildie would get a skill-up from it.
*   **In chat,** when someone links a scroll and asks who needs it, you get a note (only you see it) saying if it's a skill-up for you or your mages, with an \[I'll take it\] button to answer.
*   **Hover any scroll** and it tells you which mages would level from it, on any character.

## What it does for you

*   Decipher, Study, Open bundle, Familiar and Imbue, all one click from the same window. Clicking an imbue puts it straight on your weapon, no clicking the weapon after
*   Every scroll gets a skill-up color like professions do (orange, yellow, green, gray), and the Decipher button grabs the best one first
*   **Farming plan** (`/sk plan`): how many skill-ups to your next tier, about how many scrolls that is, how long it'll take at your pace, and **where to farm** the tier you need (from everyone's drops, and you can flip through the other tiers too)
*   **Luck log** (`/sk log`): everything your deciphers turned into, tier by tier, your luck next to everyone else's, how often each skill-up color really gave you a point, and yes, it counts how many times you got turned into a sheep
*   Your scrolls are sorted into Imbues, Familiars, Combat and Utility, and the right imbue for the weapon you're holding gets a gold outline
*   Study cooldown timer, and it knows when you're standing by a librarian. Both libraries are pinned on the map, and clicking Study anywhere else opens the map to yours
*   A little pop-up when your familiar or imbue wears off, or a few minutes before it does (you pick how many), when you have a scroll for it
*   **Locked chests:** set a key for Khadgar's Unlocking, press it and click the chest. Hover a locked chest and the tooltip tells you which key
*   **Library books:** how many of the 40 you've handed in and what the next librarian reward is
*   Open the panel in combat from the minimap button or the scroll icon
*   Scroll prices from the auction house on tooltips and in the farming plan. Uses Auctionator or TSM if you have them, or scans for you if you don't
*   **Backups**: save all your Scrollkeeper data as text (`/sk options` > Backup), so wiping WTF doesn't wipe your luck log
*   Buttons along the top of the panel for every window, plus the scroll icon and minimap button on/off
*   Hides itself in combat and comes back after (you can turn that off)
*   Three sizes: Advanced, Simple, or just the title and your skill bar
*   Or turn on the **scroll icon**: a little icon you can put anywhere with how many scrolls you can decipher (green when they still give skill-ups). Click it to decipher your best scroll without opening anything, right-click to pop open the panel in your favorite view (thanks Curnil for the idea!). Lost it off screen? Right-click its toggle on the panel
*   **Its own settings window** (`/sk options`, or the gear on the panel), and **themes**: Classic, Dark, Darker classic or Modern
*   **Modules**: turn off the parts you don't use and their buttons go away
*   A red **?** on every window: point at it for what the window does
*   Shift-click any window's X to close all of them
*   Tons of options. People love options

## Playing with other people

Everyone running Scrollkeeper shares what each scroll tier really turns into, where scrolls (and which tiers) and librarian books drop, and who needs which scrolls. It also comes with data other players already shared, so the odds aren't empty on day one. Only scroll info is shared, and you pick how: Full, Anonymous, just your guild/group/friends, or off.

**Doorways** (`/sk doors`): Scroll of Liminal Passage takes you to a group member resting in an inn or a city. Pick a town and Doorways shows who you can go to: Scrollkeeper users who offer to be a door (they invite you, the invite is accepted for you), and anyone else there, with a friendly whisper ready to send (or write your own). It never invites anyone without asking, checks they're still there before you whisper, and marks cities, where everyone is resting. Guildies and friends are listed first, and places you can already teleport to sit at the bottom. One button does the rest: ask, invite, Teleport, Leave group. (Another great idea from Curnil!)

## Alts

Log into your mage once and your other characters know about it. Your rogue's tooltips will say which of your mages can use a scroll, and there's a little window with each mage's skill and Study timer.

## Languages

English, Spanish, French, German, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese. The non-English ones were translated with AI so they'll have mistakes. If you speak one, fixes are super welcome in the [comments](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/scrollkeeper/comments).

## Works with

BetterBags, AdiBags, Baganator, Bagnon and ArkInventory (Scroll Bag sections and marks), Titan Panel and ElvUI data bars, Auctionator, TSM, and Zygor or TomTom waypoint arrows from the map pins. None of them are required.

## Commands

*   `/sk` show or hide the window
*   `/sk simple` and `/sk advanced` switch views
*   `/sk bag` the Scroll Bag
*   `/sk net` Fellow Mages and the community odds
*   `/sk mail` who could use your spare scrolls
*   `/sk swap` the scroll swap board
*   `/sk doors` Doorways: get somewhere with Scroll of Liminal Passage
*   `/sk codex` the Codex: every scroll and all 40 library books
*   `/sk plan` your farming plan
*   `/sk log` your luck log (`/sk logchat` prints it in chat)
*   `/sk scan` scan the auction house for scroll prices
*   `/sk options` settings
*   `/sk backup` and `/sk restore` save or bring back all your data
*   `/sk bug` copy a bug report
*   `/sk reset` put the window (and scroll icon) back in the middle
*   `/sk about` version and links

## Helping out

It's new, so stuff will break. If it does, type `/sk bug`, write what you were doing on the first line, and paste it in the [comments](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/scrollkeeper/comments). Ideas go there too!
